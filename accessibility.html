Accessibility

Last reviewed November 13, 2026

We want everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnifiers or other assistive technology, to be able to use this website.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. We test our pages with automated tools and by hand, and we fix problems as we find them.

What we have done

  • A "Skip to content" link and full keyboard access to menus, forms and buttons.
  • Text descriptions for meaningful images, and decorative graphics hidden from screen readers.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the intro animation, page transitions, moving cursor and other effects turn off.
  • The homepage intro can be skipped and plays only once per visit.
  • Labels on every form field, with clear error messages.
  • Color combinations chosen for readable contrast.

Known limits

Some animated effects and third-party content, such as embedded tools, may not work perfectly with every assistive technology. We are working to improve them.

Tell us about a problem

If anything on this site is hard to use, or you need information in another format, email hello@gulffreedomsolutions.com. Please tell us the page and what went wrong. We will reply within two business days and help you get what you need, by email or another way that works for you.
