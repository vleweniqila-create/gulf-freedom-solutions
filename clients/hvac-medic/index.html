Second opinions

Questions we hear a lot.

Is it worth replacing an electric furnace?

Often, yes. Big electric furnaces are expensive to run through a Minnesota winter. One customer was paying about $600 a month before we switched them to a high-efficiency LP furnace. We'll look at your setup and give you an honest answer.

Is in-floor heat a good idea for a shop or garage?

It's one of the best ways to heat one. The slab holds the heat, your feet and tools stay warm, and you don't lose all your heat every time the overhead door opens.

Can you design the HVAC for my new build?

Yes. Homes, shops, garages and barndominiums. We can plan radiant floors, a boiler, forced air, AC and mini-splits together so it all works as one system.

Do quotes cost anything?

No. Quotes are free and no-hassle. Fill out the form above or call or text Brent at (218) 820-6351.

Do mini-splits work up here in the winter?

Modern cold-climate mini-splits keep heating well below zero, and they're great for cooling too. For the coldest stretches we'll make sure you have the right backup heat.
