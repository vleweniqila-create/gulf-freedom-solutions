Second opinions

Questions we hear a lot.

Do heat pumps really work in a Maine winter?

Yes. Modern cold-climate heat pumps keep heating well below zero when they're sized and set up correctly. Most problems we see in January come from dirty coils, poor installs or settings, and those are fixable.

What's included in a heat pump deep cleaning?

We open up the indoor unit and clean the coil, blower wheel and drain so air moves freely again. It helps with weak airflow, odd smells and higher bills. Once a year is a good rhythm for most homes.

Can you work on a system someone else installed?

Usually, yes. Tell us the brand and what it's doing in the triage form, and Dave will let you know before he heads out.

Do you come out on weekends?

Regular hours are Monday to Friday, 8 to 5. Weekends are kept for emergencies like no heat, so call or text if that's you.

How can I pay?

Cash App, Venmo, credit and debit cards.
