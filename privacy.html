Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

This policy explains, in plain language, what information Gulf Freedom Solutions LLC collects through this website, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Gulf Freedom Solutions LLC ("we", "us") is a Florida limited liability company based in Panama City, Florida. We build and manage websites and run social media ads for small businesses. Questions about this policy go to hello@gulffreedomsolutions.com.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out our questionnaire or email us, we collect what you choose to share: your name, business name and type, your answers about your business and goals, your budget and timeline, your email address, and your phone number and contact preference if you provide them.

Information collected automatically, only if you accept cookies. If you click "Accept" on our cookie banner, HubSpot cookies record which pages you visit, how you found our site, your browser and device type, and an approximate location based on your IP address. If you accept and then submit the questionnaire, HubSpot may also link those visits to your contact record.

Basic technical information. Like every website, our host (GitHub Pages) and the services that deliver our fonts (Google Fonts) and photos (Unsplash) receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads. They use it to deliver the page and protect their systems. We do not receive or control those logs.

How we use it

  • To answer your request, prepare ideas and estimates, and set up your free call.
  • To provide and manage services if you become a client.
  • To keep track of our conversations with you in our customer records (HubSpot).
  • If you accept cookies, to understand which pages are useful so we can improve the site.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent spam and meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, and only so they can provide their service to us:

  • Formspree, which delivers questionnaire answers to us.
  • HubSpot, our customer records and website analytics tool.
  • Google Workspace, our business email.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect our rights or someone's safety, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

Phone calls and text messages

If you ask us to call or text you and check the consent box, we will contact you only about your request. We do not send marketing texts. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or tell us on a call. Consent is never a condition of buying anything. We do not share your mobile number or text consent with third parties for their marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as we need it to respond and work with you, and generally no longer than three years after our last contact, unless the law or an active client agreement requires longer. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email hello@gulffreedomsolutions.com and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's law. We honor reasonable requests from every visitor, wherever they live, and we will not treat you differently for making one.

Children

This website is meant for business owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Our site uses HTTPS encryption, and we limit who can access the information you send us. No website or email system is perfectly secure, so please do not send passwords, payment card numbers or other sensitive financial information through our questionnaire.

Websites we build for clients

This policy covers gulffreedomsolutions.com only. When we build or manage a website for a client, that client is responsible for its own privacy policy and for the information its visitors share. We handle that information only on the client's behalf.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date above.
